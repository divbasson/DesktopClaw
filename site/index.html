OpenClaw + Hermes Companion

Meet your Desktop Claw.

Your AI, alive on your desktop. DesktopClaw wraps your OpenClaw or Hermes Agent session in a reactive animated companion — tracking tasks, taking voice and text commands, and keeping you informed without dragging you back to the terminal.

Why DesktopClaw

More than a pet.
A working companion.

Your agent is powerful. But it should not disappear into a terminal, browser tab, or remote gateway. DesktopClaw stays with you.

visibility

State-Aware at All Times

DesktopClaw tracks the active request lifecycle. Thinking, running, completed, waiting for input, or error — it knows the difference, and it shows you.

mic

Voice & Text, Your Choice

Talk to your instance when typing would break your focus. Switch to text when you need precision. DesktopClaw handles both, relaying your input directly to OpenClaw or Hermes Agent.

notifications_active

Feedback You'll Actually See

DesktopClaw persists on your desktop. Task progress, replies, completions, errors, and follow-up prompts surface where you are, right when they happen.

bolt

Activity History Built In

A compact activity bubble keeps running tasks visible. Open it to review current jobs, completed chats, older faded history, and errors without covering the assistant.

Reactive by design

It knows what's happening
because it's always watching.

DesktopClaw mirrors your agent's real session state — giving you a glanceable read without switching windows.
